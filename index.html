Ideas for this page

Things worth putting here, whenever you get around to it.

  • Who you are. Where you're from, what you're studying, what you're aiming at.
  • Work you want people to see. Projects, writing, design, code, video — anything you made.
  • Your résumé, or a link to it. This gets useful faster than you'd expect.
  • Photos you've taken. They don't have to be good ones.
  • What you're into right now. Music, teams, games, food, places worth going.
  • How to reach you. As much or as little as you want to share.

Or something nobody's thought of yet. It's your page — that's the whole point.
